# Scalp Care: Dandruff Tracker

A calm tracker for a flaky, itchy or oily scalp. Answer a few questions, add the shampoos and tonics you already use, log how your scalp feels in a few taps, and see your own patterns over time. No sign-in, no servers, no analysis of your photos.

## What it does

- **Quick start.** Tell the app what bothers you and how your scalp feels. It builds a simple wash plan from your own products.
- **Wash day and between-wash check-ins.** Mark each day done. Forgot yesterday? Backfill it without losing your streak.
- **Daily scalp log.** Itch, flakes, redness and oiliness in a few taps, plus triggers like stress or weather.
- **Leave-on timer.** Start a timer for a medicated product, using the time on your label.
- **Photo journal.** Progress photos with a positioning guide. They stay on your device.

### Premium adds

- Flare patterns from your own log (wash gaps and triggers)
- A PDF report for your doctor or pharmacist
- Unlimited products and photos
- Rotation and flare-week routines with their own reminders
- Side-by-side photo compare
- Monthly reports, product usage goals and iCloud sync
- Three streak grace days a month

## Frequently asked

### What's the difference between Free and Premium?

Free is useful on its own: 1 plan from the two templates, the Tonight card, a side-effect log (30 days of history), AM and PM check-ins with a streak, up to 5 products, up to 10 progress photos, basic reminders and a weekly report.

Premium unlocks:

- Week-by-week purge timeline
- Custom phases and your own nights of the week
- A PDF report for your clinician
- Full ingredient overlap check across your shelf
- Reminders only on your retinoid nights
- Photo check on two compared photos
- Unlimited products and photos, before and after compare
- Monthly reports, product goals and iCloud sync
- Family Sharing, so your family can use Premium too

### Does Premium work with Family Sharing?

Yes. If you subscribe, the people in your Apple Family Sharing group can use Premium on their own Apple IDs, at no extra cost. Family Sharing needs to be on for Retinol Journey in Settings -> Apple ID -> Family Sharing -> Subscriptions. Each person keeps their own data. Nothing is shared between phones except the Premium access.

### Monthly or yearly?

Both unlock the same Premium features. Yearly is the better value. Pick whichever fits. You can switch any time in Settings -> Apple ID -> Subscriptions.

### How does the plan work?

You pick a pace. Each week has a number of retinoid nights. The Today tab tells you if tonight is a retinoid night. It is a schedule you set yourself, not medical advice and not a dosing recommendation.

### Can I add products that aren't in the database?

Yes. Tap the + button and fill in the name, brand and category manually. Barcode scan covers the most common products.

### I missed yesterday. Did I lose my streak?

If a streak grace day is available, the app protects the day automatically (Free gets 1 per month, Premium gets 3). Otherwise use the backfill option on the Today tab to mark what you actually did.

### Where are my photos stored?

Locally on your device, in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded, unless you turn on iCloud sync (Premium) or use the photo check on two photos. If you delete the app, the photos are removed with it.

### Does the app analyze my photos?

Only if you ask. Premium has a photo check. Open Compare, pick two photos and tap Analyze progress. The app asks you to agree first, then sends those two photos to our service and Google's Gemini. You get a short description of what looks visible: dryness, peeling, redness and breakouts, and whether each looks lower, similar or higher. It does not score your skin, and it is not medical advice. Nothing is analyzed in the background. We do not keep your photos on our server or use them to train models. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md#photo-check-premium-optional) for details.

### The photo check failed. What can I do?

Check your connection and tap Try again. If you see a message about today's limit, wait a day. If it keeps failing, email us with your app version.

### Who sees my PDF report?

Only the people you send it to. The report is created on your device and shared through the iOS share sheet. Nothing is uploaded to us.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Settings -> Restore purchases (or the paywall -> Restore purchases). Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy the subscription.

### What if I downgrade from Premium to Free?

Nothing is deleted. Existing products, photos, logs and badges remain visible and editable. iCloud sync is paused. If you are over a Free limit, you simply can't add new items until you upgrade again or reduce the count.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings -> Apple ID -> Subscriptions on your iPhone, then tap the Retinol Journey subscription and choose Cancel.

### How do I disable notifications?

Settings -> Reminders. Each notification type has its own toggle.

# Retinol Journey: Purge Log

A calm companion for starting or restarting a retinoid. Pick your retinoid and a pace, and the app tells you if tonight is a retinoid night or a rest night. Log dryness, peeling, redness, stinging and breakouts in two taps and look back week by week. No sign-in, no servers, no analysis of your photos.

## What it does

- **Your plan.** Choose a template or edit every phase. Weeks and nights per week are yours to set.
- **Tonight card.** Retinoid night or rest night, and which week of the plan you are in.
- **Side-effect log.** Dryness, peeling, redness, stinging and breakouts, from 0 to 3, in two taps.
- **Ingredient overlap check.** A heads-up when products on your shelf are often used on different nights.
- **Photo journal.** Weekly progress photos with a positioning guide. They stay on your device.

### Premium adds

- Week-by-week purge timeline
- Custom phases and your own nights of the week
- A PDF report for your clinician
- Full ingredient overlap check across your shelf
- Reminders only on your retinoid nights
- Unlimited products and photos, before and after compare
- Monthly reports, product goals and iCloud sync

# Retinol Journey: Purge Log

A calm companion for starting or restarting a retinoid. Pick your retinoid and a pace, and the app tells you if tonight is a retinoid night or a rest night. Log dryness, peeling, redness, stinging and breakouts in two taps and look back week by week. No sign-in. Your data stays on your phone. An optional photo check, only when you ask for it.

## What it does

- **Your plan.** Choose a template or edit every phase. Weeks and nights per week are yours to set.
- **Tonight card.** Retinoid night or rest night, and which week of the plan you are in.
- **Side-effect log.** Dryness, peeling, redness, stinging and breakouts, from 0 to 3, in two taps.
- **Ingredient overlap check.** A heads-up when products on your shelf are often used on different nights.
- **Photo journal.** Weekly progress photos with a guide that helps you hold the same position each time. They stay on your device.
- **Photo check** *(Premium, optional)*. Pick two photos and tap Analyze progress. The app describes what looks visible, like dryness, peeling, redness and breakouts, and whether it looks lower, similar or higher. It only runs when you tap it, and it is not medical advice.

### Premium adds

- Reminders only on your retinoid nights
- Photo check on two compared photos

# Melasma & Dark Spot Tracker

A tracker built around what matters most with pigmentation: daily SPF, reapplying on time, your own treatment cycles and photos you can compare month to month. No sign-in, no servers, no analysis of your photos.

## What it does

- **SPF check.** Mark your morning SPF done.
- **Sun session timer.** Tap Going outside and get a reminder to reapply. Tap I reapplied to reset it.
- **Sun log.** Time outdoors, hat or shade, and possible triggers such as heat or hormones.
- **Treatment cycles.** Enter the weeks on and weeks off you were given. The app shows which phase you are in.
- **Photo diary.** Monthly photos with a lighting guide. They stay on your device.

### Premium adds

- Unlimited treatment cycles with phase-change reminders
- Sun insights: how skin feel compares on high-sun and low-sun days
- A PDF report for your clinician
- Full history and before/after compare
- Unlimited products and photos
- Monthly reports, product goals and iCloud sync
